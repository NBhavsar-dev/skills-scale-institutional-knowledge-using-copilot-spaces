# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding support, and executive accountability for project outcomes. They ensure delivery stays aligned with business priorities and expected value.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business goals, success criteria, and investment priorities
- Approve major scope, budget, and timeline changes
- Remove escalated blockers that need executive intervention
- Align stakeholders on priorities during planning and major milestones
- Review release readiness and outcome metrics to guide retrospective investment decisions

### Goals
- Ensure delivery outcomes align with strategic objectives
- Maintain clear decision ownership for scope and funding
- Improve accountability for value realization after release

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Escalation and decision meetings with technical and business stakeholders
- Outcome and investment reviews after releases and retrospectives

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architecture and implementation decisions, helping teams deliver maintainable, scalable, and secure solutions while reducing technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and engineering standards with Developers and QA/Testing
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and sequencing needs during planning
- Support execution through design reviews, implementation guidance, and trade-off decisions
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to balance scope, quality, and schedule
- Validate release readiness for technical stability and guide retrospective technical improvements

### Goals
- Reduce delivery risk through clear technical direction
- Maintain code quality, reliability, and long-term maintainability
- Improve team execution consistency across project phases

### Typical Communication
- Technical design sessions with Developers and QA/Testing
- Delivery trade-off discussions with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Release and post-release reviews with engineering and stakeholder groups

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape user flows and interface behavior so delivered features are usable, accessible, and aligned with product outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate user and business needs into interaction and interface designs
- Collaborate with Product Managers on requirements, acceptance criteria, and usability priorities
- Support Developers and QA/Testing with design specifications and implementation clarifications
- Contribute to planning by identifying design dependencies, research needs, and validation scope
- Inform release and retrospective activities with usability findings and user feedback

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework by clarifying design intent early
- Strengthen alignment between product goals and implementation details

### Typical Communication
- Requirement and backlog refinement with Product Managers
- Design handoff and implementation check-ins with Developers and QA/Testing
- Stakeholder reviews and post-release usability feedback sessions

---

## Support/Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Support/Operations Representatives bring operational and customer-support perspectives to delivery, helping teams plan for stable releases and effective incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Provide operational constraints, support trends, and environment readiness inputs during planning
- Partner with Project Managers and Technical Leads on release runbooks, rollout plans, and rollback criteria
- Coordinate with Developers and QA/Testing on monitoring, alerting, and support documentation needs
- Track production issues and customer pain points during execution and release
- Contribute incident learnings and service feedback to retrospectives and prioritization discussions

### Goals
- Improve release reliability and operational readiness
- Reduce customer-impacting incidents and time to resolution
- Ensure production insights drive continuous delivery improvements

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews with Project Managers, Technical Leads, and QA/Testing
- Incident and escalation coordination with Developers and stakeholders
- Post-release and retrospective discussions focused on service health

---

## Security Reviewer

### Role Summary
Security Reviewers assess delivery plans and implementation changes for security risk, compliance needs, and secure release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Review planned features for threat, compliance, and data protection requirements
- Define and verify security controls with Developers, Technical Leads, and QA/Testing
- Advise Product Managers and Project Managers on risk trade-offs and remediation priorities
- Support execution with focused security reviews for higher-risk changes
- Gate release decisions for security-sensitive work and provide findings for retrospective follow-up

### Goals
- Reduce exploitable risk in delivered software
- Ensure security and compliance expectations are met consistently
- Improve security accountability across planning, execution, and release

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Control and remediation tracking with Developers, Technical Leads, and QA/Testing
- Risk status updates during release reviews and retrospectives
